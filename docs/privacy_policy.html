Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

Introduction

SparkCode ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Spark mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Code and Project Data

We do not collect, store, or transmit your code, project files, or AI conversation data. All code processing happens between your device and your own server.

Device Information

We may collect certain information automatically when you use the App, including:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs and performance data

Permissions

The App may request access to the following device features:

  • Camera: To capture screenshots for AI analysis (optional)
  • Microphone: For voice input to the AI assistant (optional)
  • Photo Library: To share images with the AI assistant (optional)
  • Location: To provide context for location-specific projects (optional)

Note: These permissions are only used when you explicitly choose to use these features. We do not access these resources without your interaction.

How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App
  • Improve user experience
  • Monitor and analyze usage patterns
  • Detect and fix technical issues
  • Send you updates about the App (with your consent)

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Server connection settings (host, port, password)
  • App preferences (theme, display settings)
  • Session and conversation history

This data is stored securely using:

  • iOS Keychain for sensitive credentials
  • Shared Preferences for non-sensitive settings

Data Transmission

When you use the App, data is transmitted between your device and your self-hosted server. This transmission is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols.

We do not have access to your data in transit or at rest.

Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (for crash reporting and analytics)
  • RevenueCat (for in-app purchase management)

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their policies:

  • Firebase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete this from our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Sending you a notification through the App (for significant changes)

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Delete your personal data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt out of data collection

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide the App's services. Since all your code and conversation data is stored on your own server, we do not retain copies of this information.

Device and usage data is retained for a maximum of 12 months for analytics purposes.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure that any international transfers of data are protected by appropriate safeguards, including standard contractual clauses.

Consent

By using the App, you consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
